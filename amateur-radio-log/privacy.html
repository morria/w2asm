Privacy Policy

QSO · Last updated October 25, 2026

QSO ("the App") does not collect, transmit or share any personal data with us or any third party. There are no servers, no analytics and no tracking.

Data storage

Everything you enter into the App (QSO logs, callsigns, notes, settings) is stored:

  • Locally on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • In your personal iCloud account, if iCloud is enabled, for syncing across your devices. This data lives in your private iCloud container and is not accessible to us or anyone else.

Your iCloud data is protected by Apple's encryption and security practices. See Apple's iCloud security overview.

Third-party services

The App can optionally connect to the following services, only when you choose to and with your own credentials:

  • QRZ.com, for callsign lookups and logbook sync.
  • HamQTH.com, for callsign lookups and logbook sync.
  • ARRL Logbook of the World (LoTW), for downloading QSL confirmations.

Your usernames and passwords for these services are stored in your device's Keychain and sent directly to the service when you start a sync or lookup. They are never stored in plain text and never transmitted to us. We are not affiliated with QRZ.com, HamQTH.com or the ARRL; please review their privacy policies for how they handle your data.

ADIF files

When you import or export ADIF files, the App reads from or writes to files you select on your device or in your iCloud Drive. We never access these files.

Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on GitHub.
